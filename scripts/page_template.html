Share of runs, not runs

County Championship batting, 2014–2026, rebuilt from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for every one of the 1,467 matches. Every column below is a share or a rank, so a season of 300-run pitches at Taunton and a season of green seamers at Chelmsford are read on the same footing. Pick a single season or the whole era; qualification and division are yours to set.

% of match runs
A batter's runs in a match over every run scored in it — all four innings, both sides, extras included. Median is the honest middle; mean is pulled by the double hundreds.
Share of team runs
His runs over his team's totals in the innings he batted. Around 9% is par for a top-seven batter.
Top scorer / top 3
Innings finishing first, or in the first three, among his own side's scorers. Ties share the better rank.
HS
Best individual innings within the selected scope; * marks a not-out.
Bdry % & rotate
Share of a batter's runs made in fours and sixes, and runs per 100 balls that did not go to the boundary — a strike-rotation proxy. Two batters can share a boundary share by opposite routes: heavy rotation, or simply few boundaries.
Pos & role
Median batting position, and the band holding most of his innings: OPN 1–2, MID 3–5, A-R 6–7, LOW 8–11. Hover for the share behind it.
Home gap
Team-runs share at his county's own grounds minus the same away. Positive means the home ground flattered him.

Home grounds are inferred from the venue each county appears at in every match played there — all 44 grounds resolve cleanly, outgrounds (Scarborough, Southport, Guildford, Cheltenham) included. 2021 is in the Division 1 pool as Group 1 of that season's three conference groups; drop it with --exclude-2021 in the script. Aggregate run totals reconcile exactly with the published 2025 Division 1 leaders. Positions come from arrival at the crease, striker and non-striker together, so an opener who never takes strike still reads as 2. A role is only as clean as its scope: Chris Rogers opened for Middlesex and batted three at Somerset, so his career row reads MID while his 2014 row reads OPN. Source: Cricsheet cch_male_json.
